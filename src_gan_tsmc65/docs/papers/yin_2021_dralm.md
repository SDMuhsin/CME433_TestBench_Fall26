# Yin et al. 2021: dynamic-range approximate logarithmic multipliers (DR-ALM)

P. Yin, C. Wang, H. Waris, W. Liu, Y. Han, F. Lombardi, "Design and Analysis of
Energy-Efficient Dynamic Range Approximate Logarithmic Multipliers for Machine Learning",
IEEE Trans. Sustainable Computing 6(4), 2021, doi:10.1109/TSUSC.2020.3004980.

## 1. What the paper proposes

Mitchell's logarithmic multiplier (LM) writes X = 2^k (1 + x), adds k1 + x1 and k2 + x2 and
decodes the sum (eq. 6); it always underestimates (worst -11.1 %). The DR-ALM (Fig. 2,
Algorithm 1, eq. 7) shrinks every unit of the LM:

- **Dynamic truncation**: after the leading-one detector (LOD) each operand keeps only t bits
  from its leading one: the leading 1, the next t-2 bits, and a forced 1 as the last bit
  (rounds the dropped bits to their mean). The mantissa x_t has t-1 bits instead of n-1.
- **Log adder + compensation**: L = {k1, x1t} + {k2, x2t} + 1 (eq. 7: +2^-(t-1)). With both
  mantissa LSBs 1, the sum LSB is always 1 and the upper adder gets a carry-in of 1, so the
  compensation costs nothing (Sec. 3).
- **Antilog + shifter**: P = {1, L[t-2:0]} shifted by L[top:t-1] - (t-1) (k+t bits in, not k+N).
- **Signed version** (Fig. 3, Sec. 3): sign detector and sign set are "performed
  approximately, which use one's complement and an OR operation with the logic one in the
  least significant bits": |X| ~ ~X | 1 for X < 0 and -P ~ ~P | 1.

The paper sweeps t = 3..12 (DR-ALM-t), reports MRED / NMED / WCE / WCRE for 8, 16, 32 bits
(Table 2), synthesis on Nangate 45 nm (Tables 3-7), and uses 8-bit DR-ALM-3..7 in a BP
classifier, K-means and LeNet-5 (Tables 9-11). For 8 bits accuracy is best at t = 7, the PDP
knee is t = 5-6; signed DR-ALM-5 and -6 are the ones run in LeNet-5 (Table 11) and the paper
calls DR-ALM-6 "the best tradeoff".

## 2. Reproduction (`src_gan_tsmc65/approx/`)

- `dralm.py`: bit-exact integer model. `unsigned(a, b, n, t)` (t = None: Mitchell LM),
  `signed(a, b, n, t, sign)` with `sign='paper'` (one's complement OR 1, both ends) or
  `'exact'` (two's-complement negation), `Dralm` (the `products()` interface of
  `mult.Multiplier`, so `check.py` and `exhaustive.py` take it unchanged). Right shifts of
  the antilog result truncate. The paper's worked example (A = 22285, B = 8538, t = 6)
  gives AP' = 1011010 0...0 (RED 0.801 %) and P_LM RED 1.073 %, both as printed.
- Zero operands: the paper does not draw a zero flag; an LOD of 0 would decode as 1 and a
  zero product would become non-zero. The model and RTL return 0 when an operand is 0 (this
  chip's ReLU zeros need it, see `aizaz_khare_2022.md` s.6). The paper's C model evidently
  does the same: with a zero flag the 8-bit NMEDs are 0.933 / 0.699 / 0.963 (LM / t = 6 /
  t = 5, as printed); with 0 decoded as 1 they would be 0.934 / 0.701 / 0.965.
- `dralm_v.py`: Verilog of a signed 8 x 8 `gan_mul8`, behavioural like the paper's own
  DC-synthesised design: sign detector, `*_log` (LOD + truncation), (k+t-2)-bit adder with
  carry-in 1 and LSB tied to 1, `*_antilog` (barrel shift of the t-bit mantissa), sign set.
  Generated by `python3 -m src_gan_tsmc65.approx.gen`.
- `paper_yin2021.py` -> `approx/results/yin2021_error.md`; `metrics.ErrorStats` gained WCRE
  (max RED) and `MRED_all` (RED averaged over all pairs), `exhaustive.unsigned_stats` a
  `samples` option.

## 3. Error metrics vs the paper (`approx/results/yin2021_error.md`)

Conventions recovered by matching: MRED averages RED over **all** pairs (RED = 0 for a zero
product; over non-zero products only, our 8-bit MREDs are 0.8 % higher), unsigned NMED =
MED / (2^n - 1)^2, signed NMED = MED / 2^(2n-1).

| Paper table | Designs | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Table 2, 8 bit unsigned (exhaustive) | LM, DR-ALM-3..7 | MRED, NMED, WCE, WCRE equal to the printed digits; DR-ALM-4 / -3 MRED +0.5 / +0.2 %, WCE 11,264 / 20,480 vs 11.263e3 / 20.479e3 |
| Table 2, 16 bit unsigned (exhaustive, 2^32 pairs) | LM, DR-ALM-3..12 | all four metrics equal to the printed digits (largest gap 0.1 %) |
| Table 2, 32 bit unsigned (10^6 random pairs) | LM, DR-ALM-3..12 | MRED -0.7 to -9.7 %, WCRE -0.0 to -0.7 %; NMED 5.6x lower and WCE 16x higher than printed (see below) |
| Table 5, 8 bit signed (exhaustive) | signed DR-ALM-3..7 | NMED, WCE within 0.5 %, MRED within 1.5 %, WCRE 75 % as printed except t = 3 (87.5 %) |
| Table 6, 16 bit signed (10^7 random pairs) | signed DR-ALM-3..7 | not reproduced: NMED -27 to +49 %, MRED +0.5 to +27 %, WCE +10 to +67 % |

- **32 bit**: the printed LM WCE 7.201e16 is 2^56, but the LM worst case for 32-bit operands is
  2^(2(n-1)) / 4 = 2^60 = 1.153e18 (eq. 11; at 8 and 16 bit the printed values are exactly
  2^12 and 2^28). The 32-bit column therefore uses a different operand range or unit than
  stated; MRED and WCRE, which are scale-free, agree.
- **Signed t = 3 WCRE**: with the literal "~P | 1" sign set, -1 x 8 gives -15 (87.5 %); exact
  output negation gives the printed 75 % for every t but moves the t = 5..7 MREDs further
  from the paper. The literal reading is kept.
- **Signed 16 bit**: the core is the unsigned one (16-bit unsigned matches exactly) and the
  sign units are one gate per bit, so the gaps (in both directions, and a printed WCRE of
  50 % for t = 4..6 but 100 % for t = 7) point to a different input set in the paper (not
  stated beyond "uniformly distributed").
- Sign units "exact" (two's complement, not in the paper) are also listed in the results file:
  at 8 bit they give 24 / 35 % lower MRED for t = 6 / 7 (higher for t <= 5) and a WCRE of
  11-38 % instead of 75 % for t = 4..7.

## 4. Student variants (signed 8 x 8, `gan_mul8`)

The paper's signed DR-ALM at its three ML truncation points (t = 5 and 6 run in LeNet-5, t = 4
the cheapest one whose BP-classifier accuracy equals exact, Table 9), plus one ablation. There
is no structured exact twin: the DR-ALM is a datapath (LOD, shifters, a short adder), not a
partial-product array, so the comparison is against `exact` (DC's `a * b`) and `mq:ilm` (the
other logarithmic multiplier).

| Variant | Design | RTL == Python | MAE / bias, all | MAE / bias, b >= 0 | max abs error | error rate | MRED |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `dralm_t4` | signed DR-ALM-4, paper sign units | 65536/65536 | 302.4 / -0.74 | 315.7 / +30.17 | 2816 | 98.9 % | 8.79 % |
| `dralm_t5` | signed DR-ALM-5, paper sign units | 65536/65536 | 157.0 / -0.74 | 159.4 / +29.22 | 1472 | 98.7 % | 4.89 % |
| `dralm_t6` | signed DR-ALM-6, paper sign units | 65536/65536 | 125.2 / -0.73 | 116.6 / +27.26 | 1152 | 98.1 % | 3.97 % |
| `dralm_t6e` | DR-ALM-6 core, exact two's-complement sign units | 65536/65536 | 115.4 / +0.02 | 113.8 / -1.60 | 1536 | 97.6 % | 3.04 % |

(`python3 -m src_gan_tsmc65.approx.check`, MRED over non-zero products; zero products are
exact for all four, `zero_hits` = 0.) The +27..+30 bias at b >= 0 of the paper's sign units
comes from |w| ~ ~w | 1 = |w| - 1 for every even negative weight; it is the net of large
opposite magnitude errors (DR-ALM-4: +263 for positive and -203 for negative weights, the
forced last bits round every operand up).

Full rows in `docs/results.md` s.5 (DC ss 2.4 ns, PT and PrimePower tt SDF at 2.5 ns = 400 MHz;
FID from 10,000 RTL images; delays from the 1.8 ns netlists at 2.0 ns). Percentages vs
`exact` (DC's `a * b`).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| mq:ilm | 33.10 / 97.7 % | 79.17 | 42,915 (+334 %) | 1.347 (+603 %) | 10.229 (+16.0 %) | 60.01 (+16.0 %) | 2.345 (+29.1 %) | 1.615 (+72.4 %) |

| dralm_t6 | 25.61 / 91.0 % | 75.59 | 7,400 (-25.2 %) | 0.159 (-17.2 %) | 8.836 (+0.2 %) | 51.84 (+0.2 %) | 1.742 (-4.1 %) | 0.946 (+1.0 %) |
| dralm_t6e | 30.90 / 94.5 % | 79.04 | 15,614 (+57.9 %) | 0.416 (+117 %) | 9.150 (+3.8 %) | 53.68 (+3.8 %) | 2.005 (+10.4 %) | 1.250 (+33.5 %) |

- **Criterion (at least one better hardware metric): met by dralm_t4, t5 and t6** (the paper's
  design), **not by dralm_t6e** (our sign-unit ablation). t4 and t5 beat exact on total area,
  mul8 area, mul8 power, chip power and energy; t6 on total area, mul8 area, mul8 power and
  minimum period. None has a faster multiplier than DC's `a * b` (+1.0 to +6.8 %).
- **The ILM question.** `mq:ilm` costs 4.3x exact's multiplier area at 8 bits; the DR-ALMs cost
  0.58-0.75x. Three things differ: (1) truncation keeps the log adder at k + t - 2 bits and the
  antilog shifter at t bits, where the ILM shifts two full residues by full exponents and adds
  three terms; (2) the compensation is free (the adder's LSB is a constant 1 and its carry-in
  is 1); (3) the paper's one's-complement sign units are one XOR per bit, where the ILM (and our
  dralm_t6e) negates exactly at both ends. (3) matters as much as (1): with exact sign units the
  same DR-ALM-6 core grows from 7,400 to 15,614 um^2 and misses 500 MHz. At a relaxed 4.0 ns DC
  builds dralm_t6e's multipliers in 8,752 um^2 (exact: 7,984; `results/syn_*_ss_p4.txt`): the
  two extra carry chains sit in series with LOD, shifter, adder and shifter in the same cycle as
  the RF read and the accumulate, so DC upsizes the whole path at 400 MHz; the remaining +10 %
  is the 8-bit core and the negators.
- **Energy.** The multiplier is 2 % of the chip power, so -42 % in the multiplier is -0.7 % of
  the chip (t4). For t = 6 the accumulators lose more than the multiplier saves: PE power is
  0.443 mW (exact 0.416), the approximate products toggle more of the accumulator than the exact
  ones. Zero-delay power: t4 8.658, t5 8.692, t6 8.718, t6e 8.938 mW (exact 8.741); mul8 0.097 /
  0.116 / 0.141 / 0.308 mW (exact 0.187).
- **Minimum period.** dralm_t6's 1.742 ns is the lowest of all variants. Its worst path runs
  through the multiplier and is 0.07 ns shorter than exact's through-mul8 path, while the RF
  write-enable path that limits exact (1.817 ns) closed better in this netlist; with a +1.0 %
  mul8 delay this is mostly a different DC result, not a faster multiplier.
- **Quality.** All four reach FID 75.6-79.6 (exact 78.65); with booth_axfa5 (78.3) and mq:ilm
  (79.2) these are the only approximate variants within 1 FID of exact. The paper-sign versions carry a +27..+30 bias per product
  at b >= 0, which by the truncated-Booth experience (bias -43 -> FID 134) should hurt; it does
  not, because it is the small net of large errors of both signs (section 4). Golden fidelity is
  lower than FID suggests: PSNR 20-26 dB, precision 0.578-0.637 vs 0.649.
- FM SUCCEEDED (2.4 and 1.8 ns netlists), PT ss setup (+0.080..+0.087 ns) and ff hold
  (+0.7..+1.6 ps) pass at 2.5 ns, GLS of seed_0 (zero delay and SDF) matches each variant's
  emulation 784/784, and 10,000/10,000 RTL images match the Python emulation for all four.
  dralm_t6e misses 500 MHz by 5 ps (16 endpoints) on its 1.8 ns netlist.
